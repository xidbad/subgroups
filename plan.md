## Phase 2: Properties of each finite subgroup

**Goal**: Prove structural theorems for all binary polyhedral groups.

- Prove **order** of each group (BD4n: 4n, BT24: 24, BO48: 48, BI120: 120)
- Prove **inclusion relations** (BT24 ⊂ BO48, BT24 ⊂ BI120, etc.)
- Formalize the **center** Z(G), **subgroup lattice** for each class
- Prove **any finite subgroup G ≤ SL(2,ℂ) is conjugate to a subgroup of SU(2)**
  - Use `avgInner` to construct a G-invariant Hermitian form (already in `equiv.lean`)
  - Extract a conjugating matrix `U` (already partially in `conj_mem_SU`)

---

## Phase 3: Jordan's Classification (Theorem 3 — PDF Section 2.8)

**Goal**: Formalize the complete classification of finite subgroups of SL(2,ℂ).

- Statement: Every finite subgroup G ≤ SL(2,ℂ) is conjugate to one of:
  - Cyclic group Z/nZ
  - Binary dihedral group BD4n
  - Binary tetrahedral group BT24
  - Binary octahedral group BO48
  - Binary icosahedral group BI120
- Prove **uniqueness up to conjugacy** (no overlaps between classes)
- Establish **correspondence with ADE Dynkin diagrams** (McKay correspondence)
  - Link each group to its ADE type (Aₙ, Dₙ, E₆, E₇, E₈)

---

## Phase 4: Finite subgroups of SL(3, C) (PDF Chapter 3)

**Goal**: Formalize Jordan's classification for n=3, following Blichfeldt–Miller–Blichfeldt–Dickson.

- **Intransitive groups**:
  - (A) Diagonal Abelian groups
  - (B) Groups from finite subgroups of GL(2,ℂ)
- **Imprimitive groups**:
  - (C) Groups generated by (A) and a permutation
  - (D) Groups generated by (C) and a permutation
- **Primitive groups with normal imprimitive subgroups**:
  - (E) Group of order 108
  - (F) Group of order 216
  - (G) Hessian group of order 648
  - (H) Simple group of order 60 (≃ PSL(2,11))
  - (I) Simple group of order 168 (≃ PSL(2,7))
- **Primitive groups with normal intransitive subgroups**:
  - (J) Group of order 180, (K) order 504, (L) order 1080
- **Simple primitive subgroups** and complete classification review

## Phase 5: Applications to Algebraic Geometry (PDF Chapter 4)

**Goal**: Formalize connections to invariants, singularities, and McKay correspondence.

- **Invariants**:
  - Relative invariants of finite subgroups of SL(2,ℂ)
  - Absolute invariants of finite subgroups of SL(3,ℂ)
- **Du Val singularities, Dynkin diagrams, and McKay correspondence**
  - Bijection between finite subgroups of SL(2,ℂ) and quotient singularities ℂ²/G
  - ADE Dynkin diagrams as resolution diagrams for Du Val singularities
  - McKay graphs ↔ irreducible representations ↔ exceptional locus
- **Further work** (optional): Extension of McKay to higher dimensions

## Technical Notes

### Dependencies
- `mathlib` is the sole external dependency (already configured in `lakefile.toml`)
- Quaternions (`Quaternion ℝ`), SU(2), SO(3), SL(2,ℂ) are all in mathlib

### Architecture
- Existing files can be extended in-place or split into new modules:
  - `groups/Basic.lean` — cyclic, BD, BT24, BO48, BI120
  - `groups/properties.lean` — orders, centers, subgroup relations
  - `groups/classification.lean` — Jordan's classification theorem
  - `SL3/groups.lean` — SL(3,C) finite subgroups
  - `application/invariants.lean` — invariant theory
  - `application/mckay.lean` — McKay correspondence
